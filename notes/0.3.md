First public build. Early access — usable every day, not finished.

## The switcher

- Cmd+Tab shows one card per **window**, not one per app. Three Finder
  windows are three cards.
- Cards carry a live thumbnail, so you pick by looking, not by guessing.
- Order follows the real stacking order of windows on screen, not the order
  in which apps happened to be launched.
- Close or minimise a window straight from its card, without switching to it.
- Space opens a larger preview of the highlighted window.

## Focus sessions

- Pick the apps you will be working with and a duration. Everything else is
  kept off the screen for that time, and the Cmd+Tab panel shows only your set.
- A session **opens** the apps in your set, not just hides the rest — it
  prepares the desk rather than only clearing it.
- The lock next to **Activate** decides how a session ends. Open: end it any
  time. Closed: ending early costs a solved arithmetic problem or a passcode,
  and the keyboard shortcut will not lift it.
- Sessions survive quitting or crashing the app, pause included.
- A configurable shortcut (⌃⌥⌘+S by default, held for three seconds) starts a
  session with your last set, or ends one when the lock is open.

## Appearance

- Two complete looks: Classic and Liquid Glass.
- Light, dark, and follow-the-system.
- Ukrainian and English.

## Known issues

- **Raising a window that was minimised sometimes does nothing.** You press
  Cmd+Tab, pick the card, and the window stays down. Pressing again usually
  works. The cause is not yet understood — if you hit it, the log page in
  Settings will have a line starting with `AXRaise:`, and pasting that into an
  issue would help a great deal.
- Sirius cannot know about shortcuts belonging to other third-party apps. If
  your chosen session shortcut does nothing, something else has claimed it.

## Requirements

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Universal — Apple silicon and Intel.
Signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple.
